Setup now says how well tested a driver is before you pick one. The signed channel carries drivers that have run on customer sites for months alongside ones nobody has put on hardware, and both used to be described identically. Each option now ends in plain words — verified on hardware, in testing, untested — and selecting one shows its version, whether it came from your own override, the signed channel or this build, and what the driver says about its own testing.
