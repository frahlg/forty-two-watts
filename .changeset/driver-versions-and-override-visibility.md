Pick which driver version runs, and see when your own copy has fallen behind. The signed channel keeps every version it has ever published, but rollback only stepped back one, so a specific older version was out of reach from the UI. Each managed driver now has a Versions list showing what is downloaded, what the channel offers, and which one is live. A driver you supply yourself is now also told when the channel has something newer — your copy keeps running until you decide otherwise.
